A short **retrospective** at the close of each [sprint](sprints.md): what went well, what didn't, what to
change. It is the concrete moment [self-improvement](self-improvement.md) happens.

- Outputs are **durable**: a learning becomes an [atom](the-atom.md), a constraint becomes a [rule](the-rules.md),
  a recurring drag becomes a proposed skill — all human-reviewed.
- **Cross-sprint failure modes** — things per-task [capture](capture.md) misses because they only show up
  *across* sprints — are flagged here and kept in one short, durable **known-issues** atom that accumulates flags.
  Three flags of the same shape = a real problem to fix, not re-suffer.
- The retro itself is brief and disposable ([run log](the-run-log.md)); only its conclusions reach the wiki.

**Why:** [pains P7/P8](../research/vibin-painpoints.md) — red tests rode through six sprints and a half-done arc
rotted, because nothing ever *looked back across* sprints. A retro is the cheap recurring step that catches what
a single task can't see.

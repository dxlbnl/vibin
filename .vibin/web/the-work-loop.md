The companion to [the knowledge loop](the-loop.md): the actual execution of work.

- **Where tasks live** — in the current [sprint](sprints.md): a short list of tasks in one file, plus the
  disposable [run log](the-run-log.md). Not a 120-card spec'd backlog ([pain P9](../research/vibin-painpoints.md));
  future ideas park lean in [the backlog](the-backlog.md).
- **A task** is a [work item](the-backlog.md) — a story (or a feature/epic when big) carrying proportional
  detail (a line, or repro + fault + fix). Tasks are pulled from the backlog into the sprint (or filed there for
  later); anything not in the sprint is in the wiki, the backlog, or out of scope.
- **Executing one** — pick the top task → [read the relevant wiki](retrieval.md) (read-before-work
  [gate](gates.md)) → do it at the [right size](right-sizing.md) (light by default; conversation or heavy when
  warranted) → [verify](verification.md) → [capture](capture.md) what was learned → close. Repeat.
- **Closing the sprint** — when its tasks are done, run [the retro](the-retro.md), confirm learnings reached the
  wiki, then the log may be discarded.

**Why:** [the loop](the-loop.md) described how knowledge compounds but not where work *lives or moves* — the gap
you flagged. This atom is the task/execution half; the two loops share steps (read → do → verify → capture).

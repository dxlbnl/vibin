title: The Rules (binding standing constraints)
type: principle
status: draft
---

The **Rules** are the constraints all future work obeys, in RFC-2119 language (MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD /
MAY). This is the one part of old Vibin that genuinely worked — keep it.

- Each rule is **one genuine line** and links to the [atom](the-atom.md) holding its rationale — never an
  80–120-word paragraph ([pain P3](../research/vibin-painpoints.md)).
- A rule is **read before working** ([gates](gates.md)) — binding, not advisory.
- New rules arrive two ways: a [decision](the-atom.md) promotes one, or [self-improvement](self-improvement.md)
  proposes one from a recurring pattern.

**Why:** rules are how a convention reaches the *next* task. Old Vibin's Rules index was valuable but had
bloated into multi-clause sentences ([pain P3](../research/vibin-painpoints.md)). Keep the spine; trim the fat.

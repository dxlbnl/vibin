---
title: Retrieval (reading the wiki back into work)
type: principle
status: draft
---

Recording knowledge is half the job. **Retrieval is the other half** — the half
[pain P6](../research/vibin-painpoints.md) shows old Vibin missed: the wiki was link-dead *and* unread
(agents only ever read INDEX).

Make agents read:
- Atoms carry a **trigger** ("when X, this applies") so the right atoms can be surfaced for a task.
- A task's first step is **pull the relevant atoms in**, not start blind ([gates](gates.md) read-before-work).
- Dense links mean one entry atom leads to its neighbours — navigation, not enumeration
  ([the knowledge graph](the-graph.md)).
- **Measure reuse**: an atom never retrieved or reused is dead — prune it or fix its trigger.

Reuse climbing is the signal the wiki is alive; a growing-but-unread wiki is the graveyard returning. This is
the **success metric** for the whole idea.

**Why:** a perfect wiki nobody reads is just today's dead wiki with extra steps. Retrieval and reuse — not
page count — are how we know [the loop](the-loop.md) is actually turning.
